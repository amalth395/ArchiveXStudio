Refund & Cancellation

Customer satisfaction is our priority. Please review our refund and cancellation guidelines before placing an order.

Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before they are shipped. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation requests may no longer be accepted.

Refund Process

If your refund request is approved, the amount will be credited to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Damaged Products

If you receive a damaged or incorrect artwork, please contact us within 48 hours along with photographs of the package and artwork.

Custom Artwork

Personalized or customized artworks are specially created for customers and therefore cannot be returned or refunded unless they arrive damaged.

Need Help?

Our support team is always happy to help. For refund or cancellation assistance, contact us through our Contact Us page or email us at archivexstudio@gmail.com.
